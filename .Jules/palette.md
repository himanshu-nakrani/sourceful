## 2024-09-12 - Missing Aria Labels on ChatArea Toggle/Reset Buttons\n**Learning:** The ChatArea component contains several interactable icon-only/text buttons (e.g. "Reset to all" source filters, "Toggle stream timeline", "Toggle top chunks", and "Filter sources") that act as complex toggles or actions but lack descriptive `aria-label`s for screen readers.\n**Action:** Always ensure nested or context-heavy UI controls like those inside `ChatArea` include descriptive `aria-label` attributes to ensure they are accessible without relying solely on visual context.
